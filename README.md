## Sections

1. **Navigation** — sticky nav with logo, links, phone, badge
2. **Hero** — dark navy, worker images, CTA button
3. **Features Strip** — 4 guarantees (satisfaction, 24h, local, flexible)
4. **About** — services list, worker photo
5. **Services Grid** — 8 service cards (plumbing, roofing, mold, tree, appliance, bathroom, locksmith, CTA)
6. **Guarantee** — dark section, 6 feature icons
7. **How It Works** — 3 numbered steps with worker image
8. **Reviews** — Trustpilot carousel
9. **Blog** — 3 article cards with thumbnails
10. **FAQ** — accordion (JS-powered)
11. **CTA Banner** — blue gradient with call-to-action
12. **Newsletter** — email signup form
13. **Footer** — logo, socials, Company + Legal columns

## Tech

- Pure HTML5 + CSS3 (no frameworks)
- Google Fonts: Poppins
- Responsive down to 480px
